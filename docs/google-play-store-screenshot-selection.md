# Google Play Store: Screenshot Selection (8 slots)

Recommended 8 screenshots for the CritterTrack Play Store listing, in order. Only the first 2-3 are visible in search results and on the listing before a user scrolls, so the strongest breeder-facing screens come first.

Related: `google-play-store-screenshot-data-checklist.md` (data needed on the demo account).

## The 8 screenshots

| # | Screen | Caption idea | Why it earns a slot |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **My Animals list** (cards with photos, status, repro pills) | "Every animal, organized in one place" | Shows what the app is at a glance; the most-used screen. |
| 2 | **Pedigree / family tree**, ideally with the inbreeding (COI) figure visible | "Track pedigrees and inbreeding" | Strongest differentiator for serious breeders. |
| 3 | **Genetics calculator** (Target Outcome or a litter prediction) | "Plan pairings with built-in genetics" | A rare feature that drives installs. |
| 4 | **Litter management** (planned, born, due and weaning dates) | "From mating to weaning, tracked" | Covers the core breeding workflow. |
| 5 | **Feeding & Care** (due/overdue pills, Fed/Done/Skip) | "Never miss a feeding or care task" | Shows the daily-use side of the app. |
| 6 | **Enclosures** (cards with images, occupancy, tasks due) | "Manage cages, rooms, and cleaning" | Shows breadth beyond the animals themselves. |
| 7 | **Calendar** (reminders and due dates in one view) | "All your reminders in one calendar" | Ties the features together; the app does the remembering. |
| 8 | **Public profile or Community** (breeder profile with animals) | "Share your program with the community" | Covers the social side, a reason to use the app over a spreadsheet. |

## Ordering rationale

- Screenshots 1-3 sell the app to breeders.
- Screenshots 4-6 show the workflows.
- Screenshots 7-8 round it out.
- If breeders are the main audience, putting Pedigree and Genetics ahead of the plain animal list is also reasonable.

## Deliberately left out

- **Budget, Marketplace, Health, Lite mode:** cover these in the long description. Swap one in only to target a specific audience (e.g. Health for pet owners).
- **Deceased cards (dove badge):** meaningful in the app, but not a good first impression.

## Practical tips

- **Use real photos.** Animal and enclosure images are now proper photos (no more placeholders); keep them for every shot.
- **One account, one theme.** Use CTU1 throughout, with data that tells a consistent story (e.g. a pregnant dam with a pedigree and a litter due).
- **Add short captions.** A one-line headline above a device frame converts better than raw screenshots.
- **Avoid personal data.** No private or identifying information in captures.
- **Fill the screen.** Scroll so each page looks populated but not cramped, and hide dev banners or debug overlays.

## Data to confirm on CTU1 before capturing

- A visible COI value on a pedigree.
- An upcoming litter with due and weaning dates.
- Overdue and up-to-date tasks in Feeding & Care (pills visible, no overlap).
- Enclosures with images, including at least one with animals assigned and one with tasks due.
- Calendar events spread across the month.
- A public profile with several animals showing.
